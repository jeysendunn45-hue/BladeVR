BLADE & SORCERY VR

Mode:
Arm:
Left stick move · Right stick snap-turn · Grip grab anything (weapons, limbs, enemies) — pull a grabbed limb away to tear it off, grab a torso to lift/throw · B / Y spawn weapon (sword, axe, spear, mace) in that hand · A / X cycle spell for that hand (Fireball, Lightning, Force Push) · Trigger cast from an empty hand · Left stick click opens/closes the in-VR menu (maps, sandbox, spawn/clear enemies, exit): point with a controller and pull the trigger. Sandbox: right stick click spawns an enemy.
Enemies path-find, read your movement, dodge, block, and parry. Block their blades with yours!
NEW: Left stick click menu now has Arms, Weapons (pick what spawns), Sandbox tools. Guns: trigger fires. Lightsaber/sword slice limbs off and cut torsos in half. Stab with the spear to impale an enemy, carry/fling it, trigger pulls it out. Red arm = heavy punch, blue arm = punch-parry enemy blades, green arm = whiplash (trigger yanks enemy to you). Story: clear 5 chapters.
Sandbox: you start with only the Spawn Gun. Hold it, press Y / B to pick an enemy, weapon or prop, then trigger spawns it where you aim. Enemies: grunt, brute (spits acid that burns you), destroyer (armored), crawler, runner, boss (500 HP, summons enemies). Explosive barrels chain-react.
