BLADE & SORCERY VR

Left stick move · Right stick snap-turn · Grip grab anything (weapons, limbs, enemies) — pull a grabbed limb away to tear it off, grab a torso to lift/throw · B / Y spawn weapon (sword, axe, spear, mace) in that hand · A / X cycle spell for that hand (Fireball, Lightning, Force Push) · Trigger cast from an empty hand · Left stick click opens/closes the in-VR menu (maps, sandbox, spawn/clear enemies, exit): point with a controller and pull the trigger. Sandbox: right stick click spawns an enemy.
Enemies path-find, read your movement, dodge, block, and parry. Block their blades with yours!
